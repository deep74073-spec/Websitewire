What is keyword density?

Keyword density is the percentage of words in a text that belong to a particular keyword or phrase. Writers use it to review how often a topic appears in their content.

How to use this checker

  1. Paste your article or other text into the text box.
  2. Optionally enter a target keyword or phrase.
  3. Select the minimum word length and click Analyse text.
  4. Review the word count, keyword frequency and estimated density.

What is a good keyword density?

There is no universally ideal keyword density percentage that guarantees better Google rankings. Focus on useful, natural writing, cover the topic thoroughly and avoid repeating keywords unnaturally.

This tool provides estimates for content editing. It does not predict search rankings or replace a full SEO audit.
