How to Play

🏎️ Touch Control

Touch the left side of the screen to move left and the right side to move right.

You can also hold your finger and drag left or right to steer smoothly.

🚗 Traffic

Avoid the cars coming down the road.

📈 Score

Your score increases while you survive. Passing traffic gives additional points.

⚡ Speed

The road becomes faster as your score increases.

💥 Collision

Hitting another car ends the race.

🏆 Best Score

Your best score is saved on this device.

⌨️ Keyboard

Arrow Left/Right and A/D also work.
