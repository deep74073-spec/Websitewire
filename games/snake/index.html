Snake

Collect the food, grow your snake and avoid hitting the walls or yourself.

Score: 0 Best: 0

Press Start to play.

Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D. On phones, swipe anywhere on the game board to turn, or use the direction buttons.

How to play

Use the direction controls to collect the red food. Each food item adds one point and makes the snake longer. Avoid the walls and your own tail. Press Pause whenever you need a break.

Game information

This is a free, self-contained browser game. It does not require an account or app installation.
