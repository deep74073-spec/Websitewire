Free Online Image Converter and Resizer

Images are used on websites, social media, documents and online stores. Choosing the right image format and dimensions can help balance image quality, loading speed and file size.

Common image formats

  • JPG/JPEG: Often useful for photographs.
  • PNG: Useful for graphics that need transparency.
  • WebP: A modern format supported by many browsers and applications.

Why resize an image?

Large images can take longer to upload or load on a webpage. Resizing an image to suitable dimensions may reduce its file size. The result depends on the original image and the settings used.

Image tips

Keep an original copy before editing. Check dimensions and quality after conversion. For website images, use dimensions appropriate for the space where the image will appear. Avoid uploading private images unless you understand how the service handles files.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

It depends on the image and where you plan to use it. Compare the output quality and file size before choosing.

Does resizing always improve quality?

No. Resizing changes dimensions and may affect sharpness. Enlarging a small image can make it look blurry.
