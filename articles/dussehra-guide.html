Dussehra 2026: History, Significance and the Story of Ravan Dahan

Dussehra, also known as Vijayadashami, is one of India's most meaningful festivals. It celebrates the victory of good over evil and reminds people that truth, courage and righteousness matter even in difficult times.

From Ravan Dahan and Ramleela performances to temple visits and family gatherings, Dussehra is celebrated in different ways across India. Here is a simple guide to its history, significance and popular traditions.

What Is Dussehra?

Dussehra is a Hindu festival observed on the tenth day of the bright half of the lunar month of Ashwin. It is widely associated with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana in the Ramayana. The festival is also known as Vijayadashami, a name connected with victory.

Why Is Ravan Dahan Celebrated?

In many parts of North India, large effigies of Ravana, and sometimes Meghnad and Kumbhkaran, are burned during public celebrations. The tradition represents the defeat of arrogance and wrongdoing and the triumph of good over evil.

Ramleela performances often tell the story of Lord Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman before the final celebration. The exact customs and scale of events vary by region.

The Significance of Vijayadashami

Dussehra is also linked with Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura in several traditions, particularly in eastern India. This makes the festival a celebration of courage, divine power and the restoration of balance.

For many families, the day is an opportunity to pray, seek blessings, meet loved ones and begin new work with hope.

How Dussehra Is Celebrated Across India

  • North India: Ramleela, fairs and Ravan Dahan are common highlights.
  • West Bengal: Vijayadashami marks the concluding rituals of Durga Puja.
  • Karnataka: Mysuru is known for its grand Dasara traditions and processions.
  • Himachal Pradesh: Kullu Dussehra is known for its distinctive local celebrations.
  • Across many regions: People visit temples, meet family members and take part in community events.

Simple Ways to Celebrate Dussehra at Home

  • Offer prayers according to your family's traditions.
  • Read or listen to stories from the Ramayana.
  • Spend time with family and share festive food.
  • Teach children the message of honesty, kindness and humility.
  • Attend a local celebration responsibly and follow event safety instructions.

Dussehra 2026: Date and Planning

Dussehra follows the Hindu lunar calendar, so the Gregorian date changes each year. For the exact 2026 date and local puja timings, check a trusted regional Hindu calendar or local temple announcement before making plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dussehra also called Vijayadashami?

Yes. Vijayadashami is another widely used name for the festival.

What does Ravan Dahan symbolize?

It symbolizes the victory of good over evil and serves as a reminder to overcome arrogance and harmful actions.

Is Dussehra celebrated the same way throughout India?

No. Traditions vary by region, community and family. Ravan Dahan, Durga Puja rituals and local processions are among the different ways people observe the occasion.

Final Thoughts

Dussehra is more than a festival and a public celebration. Its message encourages people to choose truth over dishonesty, humility over arrogance and kindness over harm. However you celebrate Vijayadashami, may the occasion bring peace, hope and happiness to your family.
