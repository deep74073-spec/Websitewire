Karwa Chauth 2026: Traditions, Puja Preparation and Shopping Guide

Karwa Chauth is a traditional festival observed by many married Hindu women, especially in parts of North India. The day is associated with fasting, prayers, family traditions and the evening moon sighting.

What Is Karwa Chauth?

Karwa Chauth is observed on the Chaturthi of Krishna Paksha in the Hindu lunar month of Kartik in many North Indian calendars. Customs differ between families and regions.

Karwa Chauth Puja Preparation

Families may prepare a puja thali, karwa, diya, flowers, sweets and other items according to their traditions. Check your local panchang for the correct date and puja timing.

Sargi and Traditional Clothing

Some families observe the sargi tradition before dawn. Festive suits, sarees, bangles and jewellery are popular choices, but comfort and personal preference matter.

How to Prepare for the Day

Plan meals and household tasks ahead of time, prepare the items used in your family's rituals, and check local moonrise information. Anyone who needs medical or dietary accommodations should prioritise their health.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm the festival date and puja timing?

Check a trusted local Hindu calendar or temple announcement, as dates and timings can vary by region.

Do I need to buy special products to celebrate?

No. Shopping is optional. Use items that fit your family's traditions and budget.

Final Thoughts

Karwa Chauth is a time for many families to come together around their customs and traditions. Prepare thoughtfully, choose items that suit your needs, and follow your family's practices.
