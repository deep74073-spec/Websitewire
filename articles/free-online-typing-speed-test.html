Free Online Typing Speed Test: WPM and Accuracy

Typing quickly and accurately is useful for study, office work, writing and everyday computer tasks. An online typing test helps you measure your current performance and track your progress.

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute. It is a common measure of typing speed. Some tests also show characters per minute (CPM), errors and typing accuracy. Scoring methods can differ between tests.

How to take a typing test

  1. Open the Websitewire typing test.
  2. Read the instructions and start the test.
  3. Type the displayed text carefully within the time limit.
  4. Review your speed and accuracy when the test ends.

Tips to improve typing

  • Practise for a short time regularly.
  • Focus on accuracy before trying to type faster.
  • Keep your hands relaxed and use a comfortable posture.
  • Learn the correct finger positions and avoid looking at the keyboard constantly.
  • Repeat tests and compare results over time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good typing speed?

It depends on your task and experience. Compare your own results over time instead of relying on one universal target.

Why did my score change?

Text difficulty, test duration, mistakes and scoring rules can affect your result.
