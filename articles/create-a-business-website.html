How to Create a Website for Your Small Business

Practical guide from Websitewire

A useful business website explains what you offer, who you help and how customers can contact you. Plan these basics before choosing a design.

1. Choose a clear goal

Decide whether the site should generate enquiries, sell products or showcase your work.

2. Plan essential pages

Most small businesses need Home, Services, About, Contact and a Privacy Policy.

3. Write useful content

Explain services in plain language and include accurate contact details.

4. Check mobile usability

Test the navigation, text, buttons and forms on a phone.

5. Publish and maintain

Connect your domain, check HTTPS and update content when your services change.
