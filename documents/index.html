📄 Document Converter

Simple document utilities that run in your browser. Your text stays on your device.

Text to PDF

Write or paste text, then create a printable PDF using your browser.

In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF”.

Text to TXT file

Download your text as a plain text file.

This page currently supports text-to-PDF through the browser print dialog and text-file downloads. It does not yet convert Word files or merge PDFs.
